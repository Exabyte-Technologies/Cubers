Licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
The database is redistributed without changes to its records; the downloaded
gzip archive was decompressed. Source URL, provider checksum and local SHA-256
are recorded in manifest.json. Attribution is also displayed by the app on
pages using detection results. No affiliation or endorsement is implied.

The Lite edition has reduced coverage/accuracy and is updated monthly.
Refresh the data, checksum and manifest as part of the reviewed release process.
Do not infer residency, identity, age or legal eligibility from a country lookup.
