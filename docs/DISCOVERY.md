# Original discovery readings and activities

Cubers supplies four original bilingual readings for signed-in learners: noticing a corner through a short 3×3 exercise, matching a complete 2×2 layer, distinguishing Pyraminx tips from vertex layers, and approaching 4×4 reduction in parts. They appear before the community feed and have separate reading screens. Labels identify Cubers authorship. These are teaching content, not invented community stories, testimonials, participants, engagement counts or sponsor cards.

Each reading has a short introduction, two or more original paragraphs, a small activity and links to existing lessons. Activities use the lesson's supplied state and verification: they do not pretend a short sequence solves every scramble, equate aligned Pyraminx tips with a complete solve, or treat a legal 4×4 parity case as an impossible scan. Physical scans remain snapshots and smart learning remains unavailable. There is no additional score, claim, completion API, timer, leaderboard, artificial activity or automatic public post from reading.

Authenticated `GET /v1/discovery` and `GET /v1/discovery/:id` return bounded shared contracts. The regional backend reads publication and bilingual titles from current persistent course content. Withdrawn/missing/malformed course entries create no navigation action, and a lesson opened afterward rechecks publication. Readings themselves remain readable when their related lessons are withdrawn, with an explanatory notice. Course publication does not cross regional installations. Current child learning consent, full session scope and ordinary account/region rules apply. Authored reading does not require enabling mainland social/game services; those remain separately gated.

Reading and card callbacks check the current focus/view generation, and the Discover view remounts on account/community/permission changes. No reading history, interests, click tracking or personal information is saved or sent to AI. Pages are fetched again on focus; these readings are not an additional offline download catalogue. Existing lesson downloads remain governed by their own publication/version rules. The original text is versioned in the core source and is updated through an application release; editable editorial announcements remain separate administration work.
