# Shareable achievement cards

Connected software milestone, 2026-10-17. Bilingual owner, guardian and staff screens use the regional backend. Fresh visual and physical-device sharing acceptance remains outstanding; the complete app is still in development. See [STATUS.md](STATUS.md).

## Images and approval

Cards use only currently earned backend milestones. The client cannot supply a score, nickname, time or achievement claim. Images use original Cubers geometry and fixed English or Simplified Chinese text, at 1200×1500 pixels with a 500 KB limit. They contain no account identifiers, dates, age, region, recordings, biography or contact details. Anonymous is the default. Adults may explicitly request their current nickname, which requires independent content review. All minors remain anonymous and require moderation; younger children also need their current verified guardian's approval of the exact image. A card request never creates a Discover post or public URL.

Each request binds the template version, locale, current earned achievement, optional nickname/identity revision, final PNG digest and approval revision. Owner previews are watermarked. Guardian/staff review displays the exact clean candidate, with an explicit inspected-image confirmation before approval. Staff must have current MFA and cannot inspect or review their own or their linked child's card. Preview bytes can inherently be copied; approvals control the app's clean export, not screenshots or other software.

Only the owner can export a READY card after fresh disclosure confirmation. The server checks the expected revision/digest, current session, achievement, identity, guardian permission and regional service gates again after asynchronous rendering. Changed identity, removed profile content, expired consent, revoked sessions, lost achievement eligibility or rendering differences stop clean export. Mainland social sharing and game-derived milestones also respect their separate operating gates. These gates remain disabled where approvals are absent.

Owners can cancel requests. Authorized guardians and moderators can withdraw an individual pending or approved card, with a reason. Withdrawal stops future clean exports but cannot recall recipient copies. Guardian activity/export includes private card metadata without staff or guardian identifiers. Staff can look up a reported request reference and withdraw an approved card; the queue itself contains pending cases. Review notices use the private generic REVIEW category.

## Sharing and private storage

The owner chooses a milestone/language, confirms the disclosure and prepares a request. A separate action retrieves an approved image. That prepared image lasts sixty seconds and is cleared on backgrounding or view/account/permission changes. A final explicit Share or Download action starts local handoff; opening a chooser does not establish delivery.

Native sharing uses SDK-compatible expo-sharing with receiving extensions/intents disabled. PNGs are written only to private app cache, with generic filenames and no credential/owner identifier in the path. The adapter allows at most four files, totaling at most 2 MB, with a thirty-minute active-app lease. Startup, foreground, account changes, sign-out and known permission loss trigger cleanup. Epoch checks reject late writes; cleanup failure prevents new writes until cleanup succeeds. Clock rollback also expires files. Cleanup can proceed while a chooser is open; files normally remain briefly after it returns because a recipient may still be reading them. If the app is closed, private cache can remain until its next launch or OS cleanup. Operating-system or recipient copies cannot be recalled.

Web uses a local File/Blob with supported file sharing on the final user gesture, or a separate explicit PNG download. There is no upload URL. Temporary object URLs are bounded and revoked on expiry/context changes; downloaded files remain under the user's control. Unsupported native modules or Web Share report their actual limitations rather than claiming success.

Requests have seven-day export windows and thirty-day metadata retention. No PNG is persisted in server object storage or sent to AI. Expiry jobs remove rows in batches and redact related image digests/targets in longer-lived card review audits; staff audits retain their existing 190-day bound. Owner deletion cascades card rows and covers related audit targets. Server rendering is limited to two concurrent jobs, fixed geometry/escaped text and native processing timeouts. Linux images include Noto CJK/emoji fonts and their packaged licenses.

Owner/guardian/staff pages fetch thirty rows at a time and render at most one hundred before requiring refresh. Cursors bind viewer and filter; a deleted boundary requires a fresh page. Immediate account/view generation guards reject stale responses. The mobile session request wrapper also rejects stale credentials and successful responses from a previous session; logout separately revokes the captured old server session after local clearing.
