# Current community write authority

Implemented 2026-10-19. Community and moderation writes now check their authority inside the database transaction, after acquiring the account locks and again before committing. A regression reproduced the previous behavior: a queued post request could persist after its captured session was revoked. The same real-lock test now rejects it with no post created.

The transaction locks the acting account, related accounts and their direct guardians in UUID order, then current consent and session rows before content. Account locks exclude eligibility changes but permit the foreign-key key-share checks needed for unrelated private notifications. A changed guardian outside the discovered account set requires reopening the action. Full session scope, expiry, verified email, installation region, account deletion and current learning permission are checked from stored records. Posting, adult club management and staff/admin actions add their own current permission requirements. Staff actions also require a current role, enrolled factor, MFA-verified session and unchanged factor generation.

The guard covers post creation/edit/deletion, exact-revision guardian approval, replies and their bounded ancestor context, social actions, reports, club creation/editing, invitation rotation/joining/leaving and membership decisions. Related content, current club state, author eligibility and membership are checked in that transaction. Existing regional service gates remain in force. Safety reporting and removal retain their existing availability rules.

Administrative post/consent/evidence/club/comment/report decisions, eligibility changes and AI configuration use current authority as well. A guardian cannot act as the independent reviewer of their own child's consent or supply both approvals for a younger child's public post. Editing resets post approval. UUID casing cannot bypass independent self-eligibility review or the club-owner membership restriction.

AI URL and model validation happens between two short authority transactions. No account lock is held over provider HTTP. Losing staff access during validation prevents settings and audit persistence. Tests use only the local simulated model endpoint and send no real credential.

Content-row waits can outlive a session even while its stored row is locked. The final check rejects those transactions and rolls back their database changes, audits and notifications. This is a commit-admission check, not a guarantee that a session cannot reach its expiry during the final database commit or after a successful response. Revocation cannot undo an action that already committed. Evidence review can append an independent privacy-shortening recovery record; its admission is bounded by session expiry, and such a durable record is not undone by a later database rollback. This does not restore publication or extend evidence retention.
