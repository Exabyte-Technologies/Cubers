# Review handoff / 审核交接

This is an operator handoff, not a submitted review message. Use the final signed build and an isolated, persistent review installation with working email and the same relevant release behavior. Production communities must not be populated with demonstration users or engagement. Never put passwords, MFA secrets, API keys or real child documents in this package.

此文档用于运营方准备审核，不代表已向商店提交。请使用最终签名构建和独立、持久化的审核环境，确保邮件与相关功能可用。不得向正式社区填充演示用户或虚假互动。账号密码、MFA 密钥、API 密钥及真实儿童材料不得写入此资料包。

## Private access / 私密访问资料

Provide reviewer credentials, account community, reachable app/discovery addresses, a named review contact and phone, and any additional guardian/reviewer access through the store's private review fields. Keep access available throughout review and test it from outside the operator network. Corporate websites and the public support email alone do not provide reviewer access. Do not activate hardware, AI, rankings or regional services simply to make a review demonstration appear complete.

通过商店的私密审核字段提供审核账号、所属社区、可访问的应用与发现服务地址、审核联系人及电话，以及必要的额外监护人或审核员访问方式。审核期间须保持账号有效，并从运营网络外测试。公司网站与支持邮箱不能代替审核访问资料。不得为了演示而绕过硬件、AI、排名或地区服务的启用条件。

## Review path / 审核路径

1. Before login, open Privacy, Terms, Children, Support and Account deletion. Check the correct regional operator, address, website and support email. / 登录前查看隐私、协议、儿童规则、支持与注销页面，核对所属社区的运营主体、地址、网站和邮箱。
2. Sign in using the privately supplied account. Confirm that production community selection is enforced by the server-observed connection IP through FreeIPAPI, including restored accounts and realtime access. Development/test use the configured server region without a lookup. Account communities remain separate. / 用私密提供的账号登录，检查正式环境是否由服务端依据连接 IP 通过 FreeIPAPI 判定并限制社区，包括已恢复登录及实时连接；开发与测试环境直接采用服务端配置的社区，不查询第三方。确认账号仍相互隔离。
3. In Learn, open a published lesson for each offered puzzle. Choose a pathway and unit from the current-unit card. In a solving-stage lesson, enter cube colors or choose a stage-ready setup. Verify that an unfinished cross is refused by the corners lesson and offers the cross lesson, that the model shows the supplied colors, and that guidance stops at the selected target. For an algorithm drill, turn the virtual puzzle, use a hint, replay and reach a checkpoint. Test denied camera permission and manual correction on actual hardware where applicable. / 在学习页逐一打开所提供魔方的课程，从当前单元卡片切换路线与单元。在分步课程中输入魔方颜色或选择适合本课的预设打乱，确认白色十字未完成时角块课会提示前往十字课、模型显示实际颜色，并在本课目标完成后停止。公式练习仍需操作虚拟魔方、提示、回放与检查点；实际设备还须测试相机拒绝授权及手动纠色。
4. In Discover, inspect the real chronological/topic feed and club controls. Use only an operator-owned synthetic review post for report/block/mute checks. Do not imply an empty review feed is a populated live community. / 在发现页检查真实时间顺序、话题及社团功能，仅用运营方持有的合成审核帖子测试举报、屏蔽和静音，不得将空审核社区伪装成活跃正式社区。
5. In Play, test enabled virtual modes with the supplied second review account. Check puzzle/input limits and interrupted attempts. Physical ranking and Bluetooth demonstrations require their own signed-device/hardware acceptance before inclusion. / 用另一个审核账号测试已启用的虚拟玩法、输入限制及中断处理；实物排名与蓝牙演示须先通过独立设备验收。
6. In Profile, inspect the real activity squares and statistics; open the gear to access categorized settings, visibility controls, export and deletion. Test the public browser deletion route after uninstalling the app. Use disposable review accounts for destructive deletion checks. / 在个人页检查真实活动方格和统计，通过齿轮进入分类设置、可见范围、导出与注销；卸载后测试浏览器注销入口，破坏性注销只使用可弃置审核账号。
7. Show guardian-managed access with clearly synthetic consent material and the operator's independently reviewed test actors. Verify the extra public-post approvals and minor restrictions without collecting a real child's identity or recording. / 使用明确标注的合成同意材料及独立审核角色展示监护人流程，核验额外发帖审批与未成年人限制，不收集真实儿童身份或录像。

No live AI test is authorized in the current implementation session. The authored teaching remains available; a mock response is not a live-provider acceptance result. / 当前实施会话未授权真实 AI 调用。原创课程讲解仍可使用，模拟回复不能作为真实服务商验收结果。
